# PR & Outreach — beyond the obvious

Vireo has two PR hooks, and each opens a different door:
1. **"A tasteful native Mac app, not another Electron blob."** → Apple-taste press & indie-dev podcasts.
2. **"AI writes markdown all day — now you can read it."** → AI newsletters & the zeitgeist.

Most of these are *earned* media (free). Pitch personally, one at a time, with the
before/after GIF. Lead with traction once you have HN/PH numbers.

---

## Tier A — high-taste Apple press (a single link here = a traffic wave)

- **Daring Fireball (John Gruber)** — the crown jewel. Gruber links opinionated,
  well-designed *native* Mac apps, especially ones with a point of view about
  bloat. **Play:** don't pitch the app cold — publish a short, sharp, *opinionated*
  blog post (your founder's letter energy: "how markdown apps got fat") and let the
  app be the punchline. That's exactly the shape of thing he links. If he links it,
  it's the biggest single referral you'll get.
- **The Verge — "Installer" newsletter (David Pierce)** — covers apps/tools he
  personally digs; free + clever + native is his lane. Short, human email.
- **Six Colors (Jason Snell)** + **AppStories/MacStories (Federico Viticci)** —
  already in `posts/press-email.md`; low odds but worth one email each.
- **MacRumors / Cult of Mac / AppleInsider apps desks** — occasionally cover a
  striking indie launch, especially post-HN/PH. Lower bar than Gruber.

## Tier B — podcasts (a solo-dev story + a technical angle = easy "yes")

Indie Apple podcasts *want* guests with a real build story. Pitch as "10-minute
segment or full interview, whatever fits."
- **Under the Radar** (Marco Arment & David Smith) — indie Apple dev, perfect fit.
- **Mac Power Users** (relay.fm) — power-user tools; a free elegant markdown app is on-brand.
- **Core Intuition** — indie Mac/iOS dev shop-talk; the null-glyph story shines here.
- **Swift over Coffee / iOS-dev podcasts** — for the *technical* angle specifically.

## Tier C — YouTube (roundup mentions convert well for free apps)

DM the app + before/after clip. "Best free Mac apps" videos rack up huge views and
a single mention sends real installs.
- **Keep Productive (Francesco D'Alessio)** — *the* note-taking/tools channel; he
  covers markdown/PKM apps constantly. Highest-fit YouTuber by far.
- **Mac Address, Created Tech, Nick Ackerman, Simple Alpaca**, and the general
  "free Mac apps you should be using" crowd — batch a few short, friendly DMs.

## Tier D — AI newsletters (reframe: it's an *AI-workflow* tool)

The "your agent writes markdown, read it" hook is hotter as an AI story than a Mac
story right now. Reframe the pitch around the agent workflow.
- **Ben's Bites, TLDR AI, The Rundown, Latent Space** — huge dev/AI audiences.
  Angle: "the unglamorous side effect of the agent boom — everyone's drowning in
  `.md`, here's a native reader for it."

## Tier E — mass-audience tool curators (non-tech reach)

- **Recomendo (Kevin Kelly / Mark Frauenfelder / Claudia Dawson)** — recommends a
  few cool things weekly to a *large* general audience. A free, elegant, "just
  works" Mac app is textbook Recomendo bait. One short, warm pitch.
- **The Sweet Setup** — curated Mac/iOS tool site (in press-email.md).

---

## Owned-media plays (PR you control — often more reliable than pitching)

- **Publish the founder's letter as a standalone manifesto** (on the site + as a
  blog post). HEY-style opinionated manifestos *travel* — it's shareable, quotable,
  and it's the Gruber/Installer bait above. This is your highest-leverage owned asset.
- **The "null glyphs" engineering post** (`posts/devto-build-story.md`) — pitch it
  to **iOS Dev Weekly** and **This Week in Swift**. They link technical writeups,
  and their readers are the exact people who'd both *use* Vireo and *amplify* it.
  This is earned PR by being genuinely interesting, the most durable kind.
- **An honest comparison post** ("Vireo vs Obsidian vs iA Writer vs Typora — an
  honest take from the person who built one of them"). Ranks for high-intent search
  forever, and reviewers respect a maker who's fair about tradeoffs.

## A news-hook framing to reuse in every pitch
> "AI made markdown the most-written language on earth. Nobody made it readable.
> That's the story." Tie the launch to the agent-boom narrative — it gives
> journalists a *why now*, which is what turns a product into a story.

---

## Outreach hygiene
- One personal email/DM at a time. Never a blast. Reference their actual work.
- Send the before/after GIF inline — the visual *is* the pitch.
- Time the taste-press + AI-newsletter pushes for *after* HN/PH so you can open with
  traction ("hit the HN front page / #X on Product Hunt today").
- Track each with its own UTM so you learn which outlet actually moves installs.
