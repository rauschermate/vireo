**2/**
> The trick: the syntax never shows up on screen, but the file on disk is still
> byte-for-byte plain `.md`. Type `**bold**` → you see **bold**, instantly. No
> asterisks, ever. Save it, open it in anything, it's just markdown.

**3/**
> Why native mattered to me: Obsidian idles at ~30× the memory to hold the same
> text file. Typora bundles a whole browser to draw a heading. Vireo is Swift on
> Apple Silicon — about 4MB, opens before the others finish launching.

**4/**
> What it deliberately *doesn't* have: no vaults. No plugins. No graph view. No AI
> finishing your sentences. No account. No $8/month. It does one thing — makes your
> markdown readable and editable, fast — and gets out of the way.

**5/**
> Set it as your default `.md` app and every markdown file in Finder opens clean,
> in a tab. Quick Look even renders in its style without launching the app.
>
