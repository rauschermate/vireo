# Naming — Markdown Editor (Placek framework)

> ## ✅ DECISION: **Vireo** (locked, June 2026)
> A tiny, fast songbird — "Swift" without being taken. The V carries vitality/velocity; the airy "ee-oh" keeps it light. Zero collisions in the markdown/editor space (cleanest of every candidate swept), most ownable name on the list. Only cost: first-sight pronunciation (VEER-ee-oh). Grab **vireo.md** (thematically perfect) plus `vireo.app` / `tryvireo.com`. The full analysis that led here is preserved below.

## 1. Brief

**What we're naming:** A blazing-fast, macOS-native markdown viewer/editor. Renders markdown as clean formatted text with the **syntax hidden even while editing** (Medium-style inline toolbar). Calm, minimal, distraction-free. "Best in the basics." Tabs, file-tree sidebar, TOC, focus mode, OS-native light/dark.

**Audience:** People who read and write `.md` but don't think of themselves as "markdown people," plus native-leaning power users who resent Electron bloat. Set-as-default-`.md`-app is a primary use case.

**Category:** macOS markdown editors (iA Writer, Bear, Ulysses, MarkEdit, Typora, Obsidian, MacDown).

**The two strategic poles this product lives between:**
- **Speed / native lightness** — the wedge against Electron (Obsidian, Typora idle at ~300MB; we're ~4MB-class, instant).
- **Calm / clarity / hidden-syntax** — the wedge against code-flavored editors (MarkEdit) and feature-bloat (Obsidian). You see the document, never the markup.

**Assumptions:** No name locked yet; English-first launch; indie/notarized direct download (not App Store–gated). `.com` + clean macOS TM class desirable but we'll flag, not adjudicate.

## 2. The "Not" bucket

How this category names itself — and where we refuse to go:

- **The "Mark / Down / MD" root** — MarkEdit, MarkText, MacDown, Markdown, MarkdownPad. The single most crowded, most invisible convention. **Hard no.**
- **The "Writer / Word / Write" root** — iA Writer, Byword, iWriter, WriteRoom. Writing-app cliché. **No.**
- **Generic tool suffixes** — `-Edit`, `-Pad`, `-Note`, `-Text`, `-Draft`. Descriptive, disappears. **No.**
- **PKM mysticism / minerals** — Obsidian, Logseq, Zettlr, Joplin. Heavy, occult, "second brain." Wrong tone for a calm basics tool. **No.**
- **SaaS suffixes** — `-ly`, `-ify`, `-io`, `-hq`. Startup-generic. **No.**
- **Literal "fast/quick/lite"** — describes the feature instead of owning the feeling. **No.**

> **Hypothesis:** We are *not* naming the markup, the act of writing, or the tool. We name the **feeling of the text appearing instantly and clearly** — fast and clear at once.

---

## 3. The ultimate benefit (the ladder)

- **Function:** opens and edits `.md` files, fast, with hidden syntax.
- ↑ **No waiting** — it's already open before you notice.
- ↑ **No noise** — no syntax, no chrome, no distraction.
- ↑ **You see straight through to the words** — the markup is invisible; only meaning remains.
- ↑ **Top rung — CLARITY, delivered instantly.** The text is simply *there*, clear and light, the moment you want it.

The unifying metaphor for "instant + clear + light + calm" is **LIGHT**: the fastest thing there is (blazing fast), the cleanest/airiest (minimal), and literally *illumination/legibility* (clarity, "you see through to the text"). Light is where speed and calm stop fighting and become one idea. The name should aim here.

---

## 4. The winning framework

1. **How do we define winning?** Become the app people *set as default* and forget is there — the one that opens `.md` faster than they can blink and shows them clean text, not code. Win on *feel*, not feature count.
2. **What do we have to win with?** Native Apple-Silicon speed (30× lighter than Electron rivals), genuinely hidden syntax with seamless inline editing, calm minimal design, Quick Look integration.
3. **What do we need to win?** A name that signals **fast + clear + calm in one breath** — and that does NOT sound like another "Mark/Write/Note" tool. The market gap is the *emotional* one: every rival sounds either nerdy (MarkEdit, Obsidian) or precious-writerly (iA Writer, Ulysses). Nobody owns *effortless clarity*.
4. **What do we need to say?** "The text, instantly and clearly — nothing in the way."

## 8. Recommendation

### 🏆 Top 3

1. **Vela** *(recommended hero)* — The most "Placek" choice: surprising but instantly sayable, ownable, and triple-congruent. *Sail* = blazing fast, *vela/candle* = clear light, *Vela constellation* = calm navigation. The V carries velocity, the open vowels carry lightness, and CVCV makes it sticky. It sounds like exactly what the product feels like, without describing a single feature — and it stands miles clear of the "Mark/Write/Note" center. Verify `.com`/TM in the software class.

2. **Lumen** *(the safe pick)* — Premium, calm, immediately understood: light = clarity. Snaps into place with zero explanation and will never embarrass you. The cost of that safety is ownability — it's a common word in a crowded TM landscape, so it's the *least* asymmetric of the three.

3. **Lucent** *(the meaning-rich pick)* — "Shining, clear" is the most literal expression of the hidden-syntax clarity benefit, and the crisp "-cent" plosive keeps it from going sleepy. A strong middle path between Vela's surprise and Lumen's safety; just check the lingering Lucent-Technologies TM ghosts.

### Safe vs. polarizing
- **Safest:** **Lumen** — comfortable, clear, universally pronounceable (and, per Placek, the most likely to be quietly forgotten in the market).
- **Most polarizing / highest upside:** **Vela** — it splits the room a little precisely because it carries energy and isn't spoon-fed. That pull is the asymmetric advantage; mild approval is the sound of a name that won't move anyone.
- **If you can clear the trademark, the single strongest *meaning* fit is Lucid** — but Lucid Software makes that an uphill legal climb, which is why it's a runner-up, not the hero.

**My call: name it Vela.** Run a quick `.com` + macOS-app trademark check; if it's encumbered in-class, fall back to **Lucent**, then **Lumen**.

## 9. Availability sweep (June 2026) — verdict flips

I ran domain / App Store / GitHub / trademark-proximity checks. **The sweep reverses the ranking.**

### ❌ Lumen — DROP
Direct collisions *in the exact niche*: **lumen-notes/lumen** (open-source markdown note app), **Lumenote** ("fast, local-first markdown note app"), **Lumark** (local-first markdown app), **lumentodo.com** ("Lumen — AI Markdown"). A markdown editor named Lumen would be born into a crowd of identical-category Lumens. Guaranteed confusion — cut it.

### ⚠️ Vela — best sound, hardest to own
- **No markdown/text-editor collision** (good), but the namespace is otherwise *saturated*: `vela.com` (broadcast/streaming), `vela.app` (Ethereum wallet), `vela.build` (website builder), `getvela.com` (e-commerce AI), `vela.org` (education) — **every clean domain is gone.**
- **Developer-tool proximity:** **go-vela** is Target's official CI/CD framework — Go, CLI, *and a Homebrew tap named `vela`*. Since we'll want a Homebrew cask and target a dev-leaning audience, that's a real (not fatal) collision.
- **Verdict:** viable only with a workaround domain (see `.md` note below) and accepting the go-vela proximity. Great name, friction-heavy to own.

### ✅ Lucent — now the strongest *practical* pick
- **No text/markdown editor collision** anywhere — cleanest in-category of the three.
- **Trademark:** "Lucent Technologies" is now Nokia, but it's a *telecom* mark, and several Lucent **software** marks are **abandoned** — e.g. *"POWERED BY LUCENT"* (software development/publishing, abandoned 2021), *"LUCENT ENGAGE"* (software, abandoned). That suggests the software/app space is navigable. `lucenthq.com` exists but is a different-category AI session-replay tool. **Verify the bare "Lucent" mark with counsel**, but the path looks open.
- **Domain:** `lucent.com` is Nokia-legacy; use `lucent.app`, `trylucent.com`, or **`lucent.md`**.

### Bonus check — Vireo (more ownable alt)
**Vireo** came back clean: no markdown/app collision. More ownable than any of the above; only cost is first-sight pronunciation (VEER-ee-oh).

### The `.md` TLD angle
For a markdown app, a **`.md`** domain (Moldova's ccTLD) is thematically perfect and far more likely available — `vela.md`, `lucent.md`, `vireo.md`. Worth grabbing regardless of the primary.

### Revised recommendation
1. **Lucent** — clean category, abandoned software TMs, premium sound + best literal meaning. **New #1.**
2. **Vela** — keep if you love the sound and accept `vela.md`/modifier domain + go-vela proximity.
3. **Vireo** — the ownable dark horse if you want zero collisions and don't mind teaching the pronunciation.
4. ~~Lumen~~ — dropped (niche collision).
